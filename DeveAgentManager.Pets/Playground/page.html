Tap your pet for a little affection. Move your pointer around the meadow and it turns to look.

Choose a pet at the top of the page. Each one keeps its own progress.

Drag the screen buddy and release to shove it: it slides like ice, gradually slowing down. A quick flick in any upward direction throws it in a gravity arc. Slower upward gestures and all sideways or downward gestures stay on the sliding plane. Holding still before release stops the momentum. Grab it again to catch it.

These are pretend tokens for trying the growth stages. Add tokens to hatch the egg and watch your pet grow up.

The chapter buttons jump directly to a stage. Progress is saved in this browser when local storage is available. Starting a new story resets only this demo.

Each pet has its own rig. The Silkie bends hand-drawn expressions with a soft mesh; Snek is drawn along a flexible spine, so it can coil and slither. Show rig reveals the mesh or the spine.

Throw up launches the screen buddy into the air; Shove makes it coast without gravity. The Silkie flaps both wings in flight; Snek uncoils and curls on landing. Both show a scared face during active dragging. Scared drag, Falling, Lose balance and Landing preview those reactions.

The growth and action controls are shared; each pet brings its own body and skeleton. Animate pauses or resumes movement; your system’s reduced-motion preference starts it paused.
